# PM-00 — first-time-user mock trials

Status: prepared October 2, 2026; **zero human trials completed**. Automated browser tests do not count as users. Recruit three people who have not seen Hauntbox; include at least one 360px mobile-width session. Use the local mock, synthetic stories and sample addresses only.

Tell each person: “Explore this demo, submit a sample ghost story, and keep the record it offers. Tell me when you feel finished.” Do not point out buttons, explain blockchain, or coach the path. Start timing when the entrance is visible; stop when they have downloaded the receipt or say they are finished. Record any assistance separately from uncoached completion. Then let them explore the curator demo and privacy inspector.

Ask these questions after the task, without supplying the answer:

1. What does this app help you do?
2. Who can read the story, and when?
3. What does the receipt prove? Does it prove who wrote the story or that they will be paid?
4. Who decides whether a reward is sent? Who can see its recipient and amount?
5. How is protecting the story different from protecting the payment?
6. Would proof that your submission arrived be useful to you? Describe an actual situation and why.
7. Did switching to the curator feel like a separate demonstration? Did you think you could access other people's submissions?

Finally test the proposed value statement: “Submit a private story, keep verifiable proof that it arrived, and optionally receive a private reward.” Ask what is clear, unclear and useful. Do not interpret approval of the artwork as evidence of demand.

Use anonymous IDs and paraphrased observations. Do not record private stories, payout addresses, identities or wallet information. Do not collect video unless the participant agrees.

Acceptance: at least two uncoached completions; all three explain the content/payment distinction and discretionary rewards. Target understanding within 30 seconds and the guided journey within three minutes, as in M1. Record blocking misunderstandings, make bounded UX fixes, and retest the affected task. Preserve failed attempts and distinguish first attempts from retests. PM-00 stays pending until actual human evidence exists.

The correct intended-product explanation is: the browser encrypts the story for the curator, who can decrypt on receipt; an inclusion receipt proves encrypted-byte inclusion, not authorship or payment; the curator chooses rewards; the operator sees the selected recipient and amount. Native payment privacy is separate from story encryption. The present mock implements none of those live capabilities and its exported sample proves no chain inclusion.

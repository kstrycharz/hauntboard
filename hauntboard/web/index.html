      How this conversion works

      Tari creates four private outputs of 2.5 test TARI each, controlled by your wallet's one-time keys. Hauntboard uses one output per post so the posting transaction can omit your persistent wallet key and named account. Unspent change from a post is held separately.

      For example, a 500 test TARI account becomes 10 available for posting and approximately 490 left in the account, minus the conversion fee. The 10 is not a charge; posting fees are charged later when you publish.

      Conversion exposes the funding account, amount and shared output origin on-chain. Later posts can still be linked through that history. This does not guarantee anonymity. Review the fee before confirming.

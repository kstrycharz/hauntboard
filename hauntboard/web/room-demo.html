Executed on Tari Esmeralda

The separate live test published a registry, delivered a recipient envelope, posted encrypted messages, removed the recipient and rotated the key. These receipts are real finalized transactions. The controls above replay the cryptographic behavior locally.

    Removal protects future messages encrypted with a fresh key. Old keys and saved plaintext remain usable. Existing board writes are permissionless; the registry controls key distribution. Titles, recipient pseudonyms, administration updates, funding ancestry, fees and timing remain observable. Group encryption does not authenticate individual authors.
